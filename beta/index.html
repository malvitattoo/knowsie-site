Knowsie scans packaged food and shows how it fits your allergies, diet and goals, with the research behind every rated ingredient. It’s the new name for the app you may know as Malvi.

Install

Pick your phone. Open this page on the phone you’ll test with.

iPhone

iOS 15.1 or later

Knowsie’s iPhone beta runs through TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying beta versions. No device registration or Developer Mode needed any more.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s free.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Open the Knowsie beta link on your iPhone, in Safari.

    Join the Knowsie beta

    Reading this inside Messenger, Instagram or Gmail? Their built-in browsers can block the link. Copy it and paste it into Safari instead.

    testflight.apple.com/join/k9gsg8Mm

  3. TestFlight opens. Tap Accept, then Install.

    If Safari shows a TestFlight web page instead, tap View in TestFlight first.

  4. Open Knowsie from your home screen.

Had the old “Malvi” test app? The TestFlight version replaces it, and your preferences should carry over. If you find yourself signed out, sign in again.
New versions Each build works for 90 days. When there’s a new one, TestFlight shows it and you can update there.

If TestFlight won’t let you join

If it says “the developer has to invite you”:

  • Open the link in Safari, not in another app’s built-in browser.
  • Check that TestFlight is installed and signed in with your own Apple ID.
  • Knowsie is rated 16+. A child account, or a Screen Time limit set below 16+, blocks it.

If it says the beta is full, or you’re still stuck, email us your Apple ID email address and we’ll invite you directly.

support@knowsie.app

Android

Google Play test

  1. On your Android phone, join our tester group. Use the Google account you use in Google Play.

    Join the tester group

    Tap Join group. If you see Ask to join or a sign-in page, sign in with that Google account first.

  2. Open the test page and tap Become a tester.

    Become a tester

    If it says the app isn’t available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

  3. Install Knowsie from Google Play.

    Open in Google Play
Please stay for 14 days Google lets a new app into the store only after at least 12 testers have stayed in its test for 14 days in a row. If too many people leave early, the 14 days start over, so please keep Knowsie installed for two weeks.
Had the old “Malvi” test app? Knowsie from Google Play is a separate app. Once it’s on your phone, you can remove Malvi.

If it doesn’t work

Email us the address of the Google account you use in Google Play and we’ll add you by hand.

support@knowsie.app

Already known

We’re on these, so no need to report them.

  • Google’s sign-in page shows an address ending in supabase.co instead of Knowsie. That’s our sign-in service, and we’re changing what it shows.
  • Scores can change slightly as we improve the ingredient database.
  • There’s no offline mode yet. Scanning needs an internet connection.
  • Reading label photos has a daily limit. If the app says “try again later”, that’s the limit.
  • There are no subscriptions yet.

Send feedback

Bugs, confusing moments, ideas. Even small things help. Use whichever way is easiest for you.

  • In the app

    Easiest

    Go to Profile, then Send feedback.

  • Screenshot on iPhone

    Take a screenshot in Knowsie and tap the preview. Tap Done or the Share button, then choose Share Beta Feedback. TestFlight sends it to us with your note.

  • Feedback form

    A short form. Send it as many times as you like.

    Open the feedback form

  • Email

    support@knowsie.app

It helps to mention the product name or barcode, what you expected to see, and which phone you use. A screenshot is always welcome.
